⚡ How We Keep It Safe
Every session is verified, timed, and monitored. No shortcuts, no cheating.
🔐
Token-Based System
Every session starts with a unique token. The chair only activates after payment — no payment, no session. Eliminates fraud completely.
⚡
Auto Power Control
Chairs are controlled by an ESP32 relay module. The chair powers on exactly when your session starts and cuts off automatically when it ends.
🏥
Hygiene Protocol
Chair covers changed between customers. Sanitiser applied before every session. Daily housekeeping checklist verified by our team.
📱
UPI / PhonePe Ready
Scan and pay in seconds. PhonePe QR at every location. Cash also accepted. Get a digital receipt instantly.
⭐
Loyalty Tracking
Points linked to your phone number — no app needed. Check balance anytime by entering your number at the counter or on this page.
🛡️
5-Layer Anti-Cheat
Physical relay gate, QR-first policy, shift-end audit, customer OTP verification, and trust score per location — your money is always accounted for.
